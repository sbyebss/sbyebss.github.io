Cosmos-Predict2.5 is a flow-based world foundation model that unifies Text2World, Image2World, and Video2World generation in a single model, using Cosmos-Reason1 for improved grounding. It is trained on 200M curated video clips and refined with reinforcement learning-based post-training.
